# Code of Conduct

TITORA projects should remain technically focused, respectful, and useful.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- communicate clearly and respectfully
- challenge technical ideas without attacking people
- provide evidence for strong technical claims
- distinguish facts from assumptions
- respect project boundaries and maintainer decisions
- avoid publishing private information, credentials, or sensitive data

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential exposure, malicious submissions, or other abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior materially harms a project or its contributors.

## Reporting

For sensitive issues, use the private security/reporting route described in SECURITY.md rather than a public issue.
